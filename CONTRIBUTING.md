# Contributing to vkmNetwork

vkmNetwork is developed inside vkmEngine, which pins it at `modules/vkmNetwork`, and follows the
engine's [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/V-KMilev/vkmEngine/blob/master/CONTRIBUTING.md) until
it has a page of its own: open an issue first for anything bigger than a fix, build and test through
the engine, and keep the wire byte-identical unless the change is meant to move it.
